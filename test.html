Welcome to BDIAS

Bapubhai Desaibhai Patel Institute of Allied and Healthcare Sciences (BDIAS) is a constituent institute of CHARUSAT established in 2016. The institute aims to develop competent and compassionate allied health professionals through high-quality education, intensive clinical exposure and meaningful community engagement.

Project Based Learning

Hands-on projects, case-based discussions and simulation activities that build confidence and clinical skills.

State-of-the-Art Research Facilities

Well-equipped laboratories that support advanced diagnostics, imaging and translational healthcare research.

Holistic Development

Sports, cultural events, outreach and mentoring ensure overall personality growth and professional ethics.

Vision

To be a leading institute in Allied Health Sciences integrating best practices of education, research and patient care.

Mission

To prepare skilled allied healthcare professionals through high-quality teaching, extensive clinical exposure and research-driven learning.

PEOs, POs & COs

Our programs align with clearly defined educational objectives and outcomes for continuous quality enhancement.

Message from the Principal

It is my immense pleasure to introduce Bapubhai Desaibhai Patel Institute of Allied and Healthcare Sciences (BDIAS), a constituent of Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT). We offer a range of paramedical programs that address the growing need for highly trained allied health professionals in modern healthcare.

Our focus is on developing graduates who combine strong scientific foundations, hands-on skills and ethical patient care. Through collaborations with clinicians and researchers, BDIAS creates an environment where students learn to operate and manage advanced diagnostic equipment and contribute meaningfully to healthcare delivery.
