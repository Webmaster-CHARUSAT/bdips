About BDIAS

Welcome to BDIAS

Established in 2016, BDIAS is a constituent institute of CHARUSAT focused on building competent, compassionate and research-oriented Allied Health professionals.

Institute Overview

Bapubhai Desaibhai Patel Institute of Allied and Healthcare Sciences (BDIAS) aims to provide quality education in the field of healthcare. We focus on creating skilled professionals with strong scientific foundations, hands-on clinical exposure, and a humane approach to patient care. Our programs are designed to blend theory, laboratory training and hospital-based experience.

Vision

To be a leading institute in the field of Allied Health Sciences with amalgamation of best practices of Education, Research and Patient care.

Mission

To prepare skilled allied healthcare professionals by high standard of education delivery, extensive clinical exposure and research.

Message from Principal’s Desk

Dr. Dhara N. Patel
Assistant Professor, Principal & I/C Dean Medical Sciences

It is my immense pleasure to introduce Bapubhai Desaibhai Patel Institute of Allied and Healthcare Sciences (BDIAS), constituent of Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT), established in 2016. CHARUSAT has been established by promulgation of Gujarat Private University Act, 2009 and is empowered to confer degrees under Section 22 of the UGC Act, 1956. Our University has been awarded Grade A+ by NAAC and KCG.

In the hi-tech world of modern healthcare, a team approach is essential to cure illness and sustain life. Our institute offers multiple paramedical programs that build technocrats capable of operating and maintaining advanced instruments and equipment. We believe that paramedical experts must possess strong scientific foundations and perform at a high professional level, while demonstrating empathy and compassion towards patients at every step.
